AnotaNiverWeb · birthday reminders, no server needed.

A Progressive Web App for tracking birthdays with colorful countdown cards. Fully offline, installable on iOS and Android, no registration, no cloud — all data stays on your device.

GitHub ↗

What it does

  • Colorful countdown cards showing days remaining until each birthday
  • Automatic age calculation — you add the birth year once, the app does the rest
  • Notifications on the eve and on the day of each birthday
  • Fully offline — no internet connection required after the app is installed
  • Installable as a native app on iOS and Android directly from the browser — no app store
  • No account, no registration, no data leaving your device — everything stored locally

Install as an app

iPhone / iPad

Open the app in Safari → tap the Share button → Add to Home Screen. The app icon appears on your home screen and opens full-screen, like a native app. Notifications work after granting permission.

Android

Open the app in Chrome. A banner may appear automatically — tap Install. Or tap the menu (⋮) → Add to Home Screen. Birthday notifications are supported on Android Chrome.

Desktop (Chrome / Edge)

Look for the install icon (⊕) in the address bar, or go to the browser menu → Install AnotaNiverWeb. The app opens in its own window — no browser chrome, no tab bar.
