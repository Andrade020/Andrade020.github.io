Peer-Reviewed Papers

Estimation of Spatiotemporal Poisson Processes with Some Missing Location Data

Nature Scientific Reports · 2026

Statistical inference methods for spatiotemporal point processes when location data is partially missing. The paper develops maximum likelihood and Bayesian estimation techniques that remain robust under various missing data mechanisms — including MCAR, MAR, and MNAR — and validates the approaches on both simulated and real-world crime data.
